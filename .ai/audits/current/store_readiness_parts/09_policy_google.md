### Per-item notes

**1. Target API + 16 KB.** Target API page: "New apps and app updates must target Android 16 (API level 36) or higher"; "request an extension to November 1, 2026". 16 KB has three official statements that don't fully agree: the May-2025 blog ("Starting November 1st, 2025, all new apps and updates … targeting Android 15+ devices must support 16 KB page sizes"), the developer.android.com page ("Starting February 1, 2027, if your app updates don't support 16 KB memory page sizes, you won't be able to release these updates"), and the Play Console technical-quality page ("Apps that contain native code must support devices with 16 KB memory page sizes"). Read together: live requirement now, hard cut-off Feb 2027. Flutter specifics: alignment depends on the Flutter SDK + AGP ≥ 8.5.1. Verify the artefact, not the version (done — part 02).

**2. Bundle/signing.** New apps auto-enrolled in "quantum-ready, hybrid signing"; "devices running older Android versions continue verifying the app with the classical signature blocks". Upload key reset via Play Console > Manage Play app signing.

**3. Account deletion.** Definition: "a unique user identity that developers provide as a user-facing feature" — phone/OTP qualifies. Web link must be "functional … relevant in scope" with deletion "prominently featured", must "reference the app or developer name … as it appears on your store listing", and must work "without sending the user back to the app"; a form or email request is acceptable. Retention for "security, fraud prevention or regulatory compliance" allowed if disclosed.

**4. User Data.** Privacy policy "must be available on an active, publicly accessible and non-geofenced URL (no PDFs)"; "The entity named in the app's Google Play store listing must appear in the privacy policy or the app must be named". Must cover developer contact, data types, secure handling, "retention and deletion policy". Prominent-disclosure examples are background/Accessibility-API collection; user-typed registration fields are not addressed. Minors: the only User-Data clause is "must not include an SDK that is not approved for use in child-directed services" (N/A).

**5. Data safety mapping.** Official definitions: Other info = "Any other personal information such as date of birth…"; User IDs = "an account ID, account number, or account name"; Device or other IDs = "Identifiers that relate to an individual device, browser or app … Firebase installation ID"; Other actions = "gameplay, likes, and dialog options". So: name → Name; student + parent phone → Phone number; email → Email; DOB, school/uni/governorate/stage/faculty/year → Other info; server account id → User IDs; app-generated random device ID → Device or other IDs; quiz answers/scores/rank → App activity. Device model/platform has no listed type (ambiguity). "Collected" = "Transmitting data from your app off a user's device"; ephemeral = "only stored in memory and retained for no longer than necessary". "You can request that data be deleted" needs "a mechanism to request data deletion" or 90-day auto-deletion. Third-party code: "You must reflect data collection or sharing carried out by such third-party code" — if FCM is added, Firebase's disclosure page lists installation ID + app version.

**6. Families/age.** Target-audience page: for 13–15/16–17, "this age group may be considered to include children in some locales"; "you must consider if they would be considered children under their local laws"; "Any apps that have at least one target audience age group that includes children must comply with … Families Policy". Families policy: "consult with your legal counsel". Whether Egyptian law makes 13–17 "children" is a legal decision — not derivable from Google. If in scope, the concrete obligations are already met (no AAID/IMEI, no ads, no third-party SDKs; phone typed, not read via TelephonyManager). Child Safety Standards: "Anonymous and Random chat apps, and apps in the Social and Dating categories are currently in scope"; a leaderboard is not messaging. Age Signals: optional beta; signals currently for Brazil and Texas. Texas ASAA in force since 28 May 2026, Utah deferred to 6 May 2027 — SECONDARY; irrelevant if Play distribution excludes the US.

**7. Permissions.** url_launcher_android 6.3.32 actually contributes **no** `<queries>` (part 03 correction); the Play declaration form covers `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` only. POST_NOTIFICATIONS: "notifications are off by default" on Android 13+; request "in the correct context", not at launch. No FGS / exact-alarm permissions in the plugin set (ARTEFACT-confirmed).

**8. Payments.** "Google Play allows any app to be consumption-only, even if it is part of a paid service"; "a user could log in when the app opens and access content paid for somewhere else". Consumption-only = "any product(s) or service(s) … cannot be purchased from within the app". Displaying "subscription active" is administrative state — fine. In-app text: the FAQ presents "You can purchase this book directly on our website" and "Go to our website to upgrade your subscription to Premium" as permitted phrasing for consumption-only apps, but (tool paraphrase — read the FAQ verbatim before relying on it) "without direct links". Links: "developers can refer users to administrative information … as long as the webpage does not eventually lead to an alternate payment method". So a bare URL to the website's redemption page is only safe if that page has no path to checkout. Recommendation: keep adr-049 §3 strictly link-free, or have the owner certify the landing page is purchase-free. External offers/alternative billing programs are irrelevant for Egypt-only distribution.

**9. App access.** "provide reusable login credentials that can bypass these requirements"; "valid regardless of user location"; "provided in English"; otherwise "the app may be rejected". The staging test number/magic codes must exist on the production backend (contract says they do NOT — part 05 API-02), then documented under "Any other instructions". Pre-launch report Robo crawler also needs it.

**10. Integrity/security.** "Starting with Android 9 (API level 28), cleartext support is disabled by default". FLAG_SECURE: only policy is that others "are required to respect the FLAG_SECURE declaration of other apps". Side-effect: pre-launch screenshots of the quiz will be black. Play Integrity: no requirement found. Developer verification: "Most Play developers are already verified, and over 99% of their apps have been registered"; "check your Play Console Home page to register any remaining apps" before 30 Sep 2026; D-U-N-S "required if you are registering as an organization". Testing rule: "a minimum of 12 testers who have been opted in continuously for at least 14 days". Zero-tap restore: "From April 2027, apps on Play will need to support zero-tap restore credentials" for "apps that support user sign-in" — plan for it. Code optimisation: "minimum of 25% optimisation, obfuscation and shrinking" from Feb 2027 only for "Apps with > 10 MB DEX code" (R8 is already on — part 02).

**11. Listing.** Feature graphic "Required to publish store listing"; "minimum of two screenshots". Content rating: unrated apps not permitted (July 2026 clarification). Financial features: "Even developers with apps that do not offer any financial features must complete this form"; since 30 Oct 2025 "you will not be able to make any updates … until you complete this declaration". Health apps declaration likewise. App name "must be 30 characters or less"; no rule ties the manifest label to the listing title.

**12. Deep links/WebView.** No obligation without declared domains; no WebView → no WebView policy.

**13. Notifications/abuse/stubs.** Device and Network Abuse forbids "full-screen intent permission to force user interaction with disruptive ads or notifications" — ordinary FCM fine. Stubs: quality policy bans "Apps that are designed to do nothing or have no function"; Deceptive Behavior: "don't include any hidden, dormant, or undocumented features" and "Apps that misrepresent or do not accurately and clearly describe their functionality". A reachable Support/Referral/Upload-photo screen that does nothing is the pattern reviewers reject under Broken Functionality.

**14. 2025–2026 changes.** Announcements: 10 Jul 2025 (API 35), 30 Oct 2025 (Age-Restricted Content; Financial declaration blocks updates; Accessibility API), 9 Dec 2025 (US billing), 15 Apr 2026 (Contacts picker; Account Transfer; incidental-dating), 15 Jul 2026 (anonymous-chat; READ_CALL_LOG; developer-verification registration; User Data covers third-party AI; unrated apps; Data-safety location guidance). Android 16 for target 36: "your app can't opt-out of going edge-to-edge" — Flutter ≥3.27 defaults to `SystemUiMode.edgeToEdge`; predictive back: "onBackPressed is not called and KeyEvent.KEYCODE_BACK is not dispatched anymore" — Flutter needs `android:enableOnBackInvokedCallback="true"` and `PopScope`; memory's "BACK key kills the app" should be re-checked. Large screens ≥600 dp ignore `screenOrientation`. `elegantTextHeight` deprecation affects Arabic in native views only.
