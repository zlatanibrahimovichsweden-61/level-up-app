### Entitlement lifecycle — contract, then app trace

**Contract.** `GET /me/entitlements` (`openapi.yaml:1065-1090`): stable, "Refetch on app resume. There is no `pending` state — an entitlement is active or it isn't", returns `{items: EntitlementState[]}`. `EntitlementState` (`:2444-2451`) = `type` (`EntitlementType` enum `[baseSubscription, flashcardSubscription]`, extensible, `:2440-2443`), `active: bool`, `expiresAt: date-time|null`, `inGrace: bool` ("2-day grace after expiry"), `graceEndsAt: date-time|null`; required `[type, active, inGrace]`. `StudentProfile.entitlements` is a "read-only embed for first paint" (`:2425-2428`). The only refetch trigger the contract names is resume; the old "on checkout deep-link return" was deleted at 2.2.0-m1 (`changelog:317`). Enforcement codes: `403 subscription_required` (createQuiz `:1455`, getExplanation `:1629-1632`), `403 flashcard_subscription_required` (getFlashcardsForLecture `:1803`, university-only, free weekly preview truncation `:1746-1749`), `403 weekly_question_limit_reached` = "Free-plan 50 questions/week exhausted… resets at the start of the next week" (`error-codes.md`), quiz-only (`:1813-1814`). Note `changelog:166` (3.9.2-m1) called `getMyEntitlements` "published-stable-but-unrouted"; staging today answers `401` rather than `404`, so a route now appears to exist (STAGING inference only).

### DeviceInfo — what device data leaves the phone

Contract `DeviceInfo` (`openapi.yaml:2239-2247`): `deviceId` (string ≤64, required, "client-generated STABLE id… regenerated on reinstall"), `platform` (enum `android|ios`, required), `model` (string ≤100, optional, "display label for eviction messaging"). App (`device_info.dart:14-19`, `device_info_provider.dart:15-28`): `deviceId` = 32-hex from `Random.secure()`, generated once and kept in secure storage, survives logout by design (`secure_storage.dart:72-83`) — **not** a hardware identifier (no ANDROID_ID/IDFV/IDFA); `platform` from `dart:io`; `model` = `device_info_plus` `androidInfo.model` / `iosInfo.model`. Sent on `verifyOtp` (`auth_repository.dart:58-60`) and inside both registration bodies; `deviceId` alone on `/auth/refresh` (`:86-88`). Not sent: OS version, app version, locale, push token, advertising IDs.

### Auth contract summary (for the reviewer-flow section)

Phone: E.164 `^\+[1-9][0-9]{7,14}$` (`:2236-2237`) — not Egypt-only in the contract; the app fixes a `+20` chip and normalises `010…` → `+2010…` (`phone.dart:17-43`). OTP: 6 digits (`:189`), 5-min expiry, 60 s resend, 5 failed verifies → 15-min lock `otp_throttled` (`:86-88`); `requestOtp` always `202` (anti-enumeration) and "silently ignored for abuse-flagged numbers" (`:141-156`); `429 otp_throttled` "cooldown or per-identifier/device/IP limit" (`:169`) with **no `Retry-After` declared** — timing comes from `resendAfterSeconds`/`expiresInSeconds` in the `202` body; `Retry-After` is only promised on `rate_limited` and the app parses delta-seconds only (`error_interceptor.dart:65-70`). Tokens: access JWT ~15 min (`accessExpiresInSeconds` 900), refresh rotating, TTL 60 days (5 184 000 s), reuse detection revokes the whole chain → `refresh_token_invalid`; new-device verify evicts the old session → `session_superseded` on any endpoint (`:56-60, 180-181, 401-403`); `registrationToken` 10 min, device-bound. Reviewer mechanism: staging-only (API-02).
